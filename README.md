# GDG WOW Pune

Welcome to the official repository for Google Developer Group Women of Wearables (WOW) Pune!

## About Us

GDG WOW Pune is a community-driven initiative focused on empowering women in technology, particularly in the fields of wearable technology, IoT, and mobile development. We organize workshops, hackathons, tech talks, and networking events to foster learning and collaboration among developers in Pune.

## Our Mission

- Create an inclusive tech community for women and allies in Pune
- Share knowledge and resources about Google technologies
- Provide mentorship and growth opportunities
- Bridge the gender gap in technology
